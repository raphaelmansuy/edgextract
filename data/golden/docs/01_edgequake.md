# EdgeQuake

EdgeQuake is a Graph-RAG product created by a small engineering team.
It stores entities in **PostgreSQL** with **Apache AGE** and **pgvector**.

In 2024, Jane Doe joined Acme Inc. as VP Engineering in Berlin.
Acme Inc uses EdgeQuake to index markdown documentation.

Nimble is a decision model. Ollama serves Nimble on a local machine.
EdgeQuake depends on PostgreSQL.
